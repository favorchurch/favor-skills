# Favor QA Guidelines Reference

Use this reference only when the user asks for a detailed QA pass, social media QA, design/video requirements, or a fuller Favor standards check.

Source document: QA Guidelines [ao 2025]
Google Doc: docs.google.com/document/d/1AFhChY-cTd7azlTdWxEvZDd2ki467V5J5dMAsG7SZLc/edit?tab=t.0

## Why QA matters

Favor QA aims to reduce distractions and make communication clear, readable, understandable, and excellent. Materials should be checked before posting or distribution, especially anything going to social media platforms, groups, channels, email lists, large Facebook groups, or public/internal printed materials.

## Self-check before requesting QA

- Study the QA Guidelines.
- Complete Basecamp templates when requesting work from Crtvs, Marketing, and Tech teams.
- Check grammar and spelling.
- Confirm permission to use content, graphics, audio, and footage, especially content involving kids or minors.
- Include credits when needed.
- Avoid plagiarism. When in doubt, ask.

## Writing style highlights

- `Church`: universal body of believers.
- `church`: building, service, or organization.
- `Connect Group`: first mention. `Connect` is acceptable after.
- `Connect leaders` when talking about them. `Connect Leaders` when talking to them.
- `livestream` as noun. `live-stream` as verb.
- `Ps`: Pastor abbreviation, no period.
- `PS.`: postscript.
- Limit capitalization for emphasis.
- Do not use Title Case for email subjects unless needed by the concept.
- Use bullet points instead of hyphen lists or numbered lists unless listing steps.
- Use Oxford comma.
- Use `1990s` or `‘90s`, never `1990’s` or `90’s`.

## Dates, times, and event details

- Use `Jan 1` or `January 1, 2020`.
- Never use `Jan 1st` or `1st of Jan`.
- Use `AM` and `PM` with no space: `7AM`, `7PM`, `7:15PM`.
- Do not write `7:00PM` unless a platform requires it.
- Invite format: day, date, time, venue.

Example:

```text
Saturday, March 8, 10AM
Favor Studio, Shangri-La Plaza
```

## URLs and forms

- Do not show `http://`, `https://`, or `www` before web addresses unless required by the platform.
- Use lowercase links unless case-sensitive.
- For long public links, request a `favor.church` shortlink if it will be used long-term.
- For temporary internal links, `tinyurl.com` or `bit.ly` is acceptable.
- For form links, use `favor.church/mnl/form/{id}` where `{id}` comes from the Fluro share link.

## Voice and tone

Favor Church main voice: vibrant, authentic, accepting, relevant, and culture-shaping.

Tone by context:

- Inviting: casual, not desperate. Use `Join us!`, not `Please join us!`.
- Explaining, teaching, or correcting: truthful, encouraging, patient, non-judgmental, loving, understanding, genuine, and not preachy.
- Welcoming: friendly and casual.
- Leaders and volunteers: clear, motivating, encouraging, understanding, fun, and team-oriented.
- Visitors: friendly, non-threatening, and clear.

Favor Kids voice: fun, friendly, vibrant, and passionate about Jesus. Speak to parents with clarity, trustworthiness, empathy, and helpfulness.

## Social media QA request format

Use this structure when preparing a QA post:

```text
<#CLUSTER> <Description here>
Target post date/time: Wed, April 29, 9AM
Cut-off date/time for comments: April 28, 5:30PM
Notes: <Additional remarks>
```

Clusters include `#DEAF`, `#FAVORGIRL`, `#KIDS`, `#MAIN`, `#YOUTH`, and `#MOVEMENT`.

Include what the material is and where it will be posted or sent, for example FB/IG feed post, FB/IG story, parent email, Fluro email, recap caption, or captions for all platforms.

## Video QA highlights

- Standard upload frame rate: 24fps or 23.976fps.
- Label links correctly if included in CBB.
- Keep story text within margins so it is not cut off on Android phones.
- Use proportional margins unless intentional.
- Avoid red audio meters. In general, keep audio below -12 on the meters.
- Cut on good beats and remove awkward pauses, fillers, dead air, and distracting expressions where possible.
- For videos with kids, confirm parent or guardian permission or blur faces.
- Avoid distracting ads, alcohol, cigarettes, vapes, or inappropriate settings in footage.
- Make subtitles readable in font size, color, font choice, and background.
- Color grade and color-correct as needed.

Preview codec guidance:

```text
H.264, 1280x720p, 30fps, VBR 1 Pass, Target Bitrate 2mbps
```

Facebook restream optimal settings:

```text
Video: H.264, 1920x1080p, 24fps, Constant Bit Rate 5mbps, keyframe distance 2s
Audio: AAC, 44.1khz, Stereo, 160kbps
```

## Reels QA highlights

- Make sure the video is clear and the camera lens is clean.
- Prepare a reel cover.
- Make sure the audio is clear and properly mixed.
- Cut on good beats and remove awkward pauses, fillers, and dead air when possible.
- Confirm permission for kids or blur faces.
- Use a filter that fits the audience and content.
- Make subtitles readable.

## Graphics QA highlights

- Fonts must be readable on mobile.
- Event graphics must include essential information: date, day, time, and venue. For online or Zoom events, include the URL unless private.
- Never use ordinal dates like `May 3rd`. Use `May 3`.
- For shareable FB/IG stories, include the church or ministry logo.
- Do not squish or stretch the logo.
- Do not add outer glow, under shadow, or special effects around the logo.
- Keep story text within margins so it is not cut off on Android phones.

## Photo QA highlights

- Photos should be vibrant but not too saturated.
- Crop out unnecessary details.
- Remember the rule of thirds.
- For Main Church, Favor Girl, Favor Men, and Favor Business, show diversity of age, gender, and ethnicity when using attendee photos.
- For Favor Youth and Young Adults, show a balance of guys and girls.
- For Kids, confirm permission, balance boys and girls, and remove or blur name tags.
- Do not use sad, angry, or masungit-looking photos.

## Email and copy QA highlights

- Avoid Christianese. Copy should be understandable to an unchurched person.
- Sound genuine, patient, casual, friendly, and fun when applicable.
- Avoid sounding preachy or judgmental.
- Prefer `we` over `you` where natural.
- Check theological accuracy.
- Simpler is often better.
- If referring to God, Jesus, or the Holy Spirit in captions, use their names in the first sentence, then lowercase pronouns after.
- Include any hashtags that will go on captions.
