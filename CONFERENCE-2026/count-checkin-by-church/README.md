# count-checkin-by-church

Add a **normalized, self-updating per-church check-in count** to the conference live-count Google Sheet, and keep the "unique churches checked in" number (SUMMARY!C14) honest.

---

## What it does

- Adds a **`CHECK-IN BY CHURCH`** tab: one row per church with how many attendees checked in (`"Yes"`), counted **once per normalized church** (spelling variants folded), plus a live summary (unique / matched / unmatched / Favor / total).
- Adds a **`CHURCH LOOKUP`** tab: the flat `nkey → "Name | City"` map that drives the normalization, generated by the bundled `build_lookup.py`.
- Reads **every** church field (`N` + fallbacks `S`/`T`/`AH`/`AI`), not just column N.
- Repoints **`SUMMARY!C14`** to the correct normalized count.
- **Live:** the tab recomputes on its own each time MASTERLIST is re-pasted — only the lookup needs a periodic refresh.

It reuses the normalized list maintained by [`update-unique-churches`](../update-unique-churches/) as the single source of truth for matching — it never re-invents normalization.

Full workflow, the exact formulas, and gotchas in [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md).

---

## The bug it fixes

`SUMMARY!C14` used `COUNTUNIQUEIFS` on raw column N: it **over-counted** spelling variants (256 raw vs ~157 normalized), **under-counted** by ignoring the fallback church columns (~⅓ of check-ins), and counted junk as churches. This skill fixes all three.

---

## How to use it

- **Slash command:** `/count-checkin-by-church`
- **Plain language:** "count check-ins per church", "how many churches checked in?", "refresh the check-in lookup", "double-check the C14 church check-in count".

---

## Recipes

| Recipe | What it does |
|---|---|
| *"How many churches have checked in?"* | Reads the live `CHECK-IN BY CHURCH` summary and reports the normalized unique count + per-church leaders. |
| *"Refresh the check-in lookup."* | Runs `update-unique-churches`, regenerates `CHURCH LOOKUP`, and the live tab + C14 recompute. |
| *"Double-check SUMMARY!C14."* | Explains the raw-vs-normalized gap and repoints C14 to the correct number. |
| *"Set up the check-in-by-church tab."* | First-time build: creates both tabs, writes the formulas, wires C14. |

---

## Notes

- **`⚠` rows** on the tab are raw strings not yet in the lookup (mostly junk names) — a review flag, already excluded from the unique count. Refresh to fold in any that are real churches.
- The count **moves during the event** — it's a live count; the formulas track the current MASTERLIST.
- Favor-location attendees are counted separately (not in the per-church list), matching the church-count scope.
