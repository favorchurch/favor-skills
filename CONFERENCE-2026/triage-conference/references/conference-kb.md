## 1. Event facts

- **Event:** Favor Conference 2026
- **Dates:** July 2–4, 2026 (Thu–Sat). Sessions run Thu 6PM start through Sat 9PM.

## 2. Tickets & pricing

- **Adult:** Regular ₱1000 (Super Early Bird ₱500 / Early Bird ₱750 — both closed).
- **Student:** Regular ₱250 (Super Early Bird ₱150 / Early Bird ₱200 — both closed).
- **Kids:** ₱200 (ages 1–12, includes free dinner Fri & Sat).

## 3. Common email scenarios → how to answer (matches Rico's reply patterns)

| Inbound intent | Answer + action |
|---|---|
| **"I didn't receive my ticket / QR"** | Find their attendee record, **resend** the QR ticket, then reply: ticket resent to [email], please check inbox **and spam/junk**. |
| **"Is my order confirmation my ticket?"** | Clarify: the payment/order confirmation email is just the order confirmation; the **actual QR tickets are sent separately** to each attendee's email. Offer to resend. |
| **Wrong/bounced email** | Correct the email in Favor Event Tickets, resend, confirm to sender. |
| **Wants to transfer a ticket** | Point to self-service form **favor.church/tickettransfer**. Remind: non-refundable but transferable **until June 26**. (Kanban handles the form submissions.) |
| **Can't afford / financial assistance** | Point to **favor.church/financialaid**, deadline **June 26**. Coupon workflow follows (see §4). |
| **Multiple-ticket financial aid** | Needs approval. Forward to **Ps Dawn & Kim** before issuing more than one coupon. |
| **Wants to serve / Open Access volunteer** | Point to **favor.church/serveatconference**; the team follows up with huddles + personal roster. Share the Open Access schedule if asked. |
| **Sends Favor DNA proof/screenshot** | Acknowledge + tag their Fluro contact (see §10). |
| **Sends an ID with no context** | Ask what it's in reference to (financial aid, ticket verification, etc.) before acting. |
| **Sponsorship / donation** | Point to **sponsorships@favor.church**. Sponsored tickets go to anyone in need unless they name a specific recipient. |
| **Kids: helper/yaya accompanying child** | Acknowledge; note the helper must stay with the child the entire time. |
| **BTS session registration** | BTS sessions are part of Open Access (free, for pastors/leaders/teams). Make sure they have their QR ticket first. See §6. |

## 4. Financial assistance (coupon workflow)

- Form: **favor.church/financialaid**, deadline **June 26**.
- Applicant states ticket type + how much they can pay; a coupon code covers the rest.
- Coupon reply pattern: "Here is your coupon code for Favor Conference 2026 worth ₱X off: [CODE]. Your total amount to pay is ₱Y. This is **not your official ticket yet** — follow the steps to claim it."
- **Multiple tickets** in one request: get sign-off from **Ps Dawn & Kim** before issuing more than one coupon.

- Each child gets a **wristband** valid for all 3 days; replacements available.
- **Registration / session locations:** Ages 1–3 at Dugout D (beside left entrance); Preschool–Gr 6 at Makabang San Juan Basketball Court, 4th Floor.
- **Pickup windows:** Thu 8:50–10PM; Fri 5:30–7PM (optional dinner with kids, check-in reopens 6:30PM) and 9:30–10:30PM; Sat 12–1PM (optional lunch, reopens 2PM) and 7–7:30PM.
- Nursing area + baby play space onsite; bring your own insulated storage for breastmilk (no fridge). No food/drinks inside the arena.
- Strollers: guidelines TBD; ramps/accessible pathways available.
- Concerns onsite: Edward Barber 0917 850 0584 / Karl Olaño 0936 935 5634.

3. **Tag the contact** with the graduate tag **🎓 Favor DNA** — Fluro tag `_id`: `6824574fea78c50036f1b7a4` (definition `equipping`). (Do **not** use the plain "Favor DNA" tag `687641e7d93dae0036d7e48c`, which is the in-progress tag.)

4. Confirm the tag was applied before marking the email handled.
